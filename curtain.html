⚠️ challenge and solution

During the development process, I encountered several technical difficulties:

🎨 Complete compatibility with dark themes

It is necessary to ensure that all elements have appropriate colors in the dark mode.

🔍 see solution
✅ solution:

  • Use CSS variables to uniformly manage colors.
